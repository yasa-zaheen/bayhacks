ACT 1 — FRONT (BLUR)
--------------------
Voice / action (deterministic):

  1. Tap START
     → welcome.mp3
     → step 1.mp3  (“Step one — the front…”)

  2. ON PURPOSE: shake the device 2–3 seconds while ring fills
     → auto-capture fires
     → OpenCV: issue blur
     → retake_blur.mp3
     → Retake compare shows REJECTED (blur)

  3. Hold completely still until ring fills from zero
     → hold steady.mp3 → nice picture.mp3
     → capture passes (live QC mode)
     → retake_success.mp3
     → Front view locked ✓

Say to camera:
  “ApertureGrade rejected motion blur, coached a retake, and accepted a sharp front
   shot — same OpenCV rules as the practice dataset.”


ACT 2 — REAR / PORTS (TOO DARK)
-------------------------------
Voice / action:

  4. back.mp3  (“Step two — the back, where all the ports and details are.”)

  5. ON PURPOSE: turn laptop so rear is in shadow / away from window
     → capture fails
     → OpenCV: underexposed
     → retake_dark.mp3  (“The contrast is too dark…”)

  6. Move rear toward window or desk lamp — hold still
     → capture the rear.mp3
     → retake_success.mp3
     → Rear view locked ✓

Say to camera:
  “Lighting is checked before blur — the coach tells them to brighten, not
   just ‘try again’.”


ACT 3 — SERIAL / ASSET TAG (GLARE + TOO CLOSE)
----------------------------------------------
Voice / action:

  7. hold close.mp3 or serial tag.mp3  (“Step three — serial number…”)

  8. ON PURPOSE: tilt bottom sticker toward ceiling light
     → capture fails
     → OpenCV: glare_or_overexposed
     → retake_glare.mp3

  9. ON PURPOSE: move camera too close — tag clips frame
     → capture fails
     → OpenCV: framing
     → retake_framing.mp3  (“You're too close. Step back…”)

 10. Step back ~30 cm, angle to kill glare, hold still
     → hold close.mp3
     → capture passes
     → retake_success.mp3
     → all photos captured.mp3
     → Celebration overlay
     → Passport opens automatically
