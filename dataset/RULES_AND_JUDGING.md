# Rules and judging

Proposed detailed rules for organizer review before kickoff. The selected track, $500 cash prize and optional live camera are confirmed; the scoring weights and submission logistics below are not yet published agreements.

## Building your entry

Build your application, AI workflow and interface during the hackathon. Follow Bay Hacks' event rules for existing models, APIs, libraries, collaboration and previously created work. Disclose external tools and what your team contributed. We supply photographs, examples and documentation, not starter application code.

Account for any external API's access requirements and limits, including those applicable to younger participants. ACS compute, accounts and paid API credits are not promised. Explain any service receiving images and the retention settings you use. A low-cost or local approach is welcome; spending more does not earn points by itself.

Keep source photographs intact. You may resize or preprocess working copies for analysis, but show the original being evaluated. Do not pass an enhanced image off as a successful original capture. Do not fabricate label text or claim to verify ownership, device identity, data erasure or compliance.

Use supplied or otherwise permitted materials. Do not bring customer photographs, personal information, credentials or production records into the challenge. There is no requirement to enter an ACS facility or access an ACS production system.

## Proposed scoring: 100 points

| Criterion | Maximum | What earns points |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Correct and useful feedback | 30 | Detects visible defects and missing views while accepting usable photos |
| Clear retake guidance | 25 | Specific, understandable instructions suited to the actual problem |
| Working prototype | 20 | Reliable photo input, evaluation and output, including additional judge examples |
| Thoughtful use of AI | 15 | Useful model contribution, clear explanation of rules versus AI, honest limitations |
| Usability and presentation | 10 | A workflow an operator can understand and use |

Judges assess the published photo checks: blur, lighting, framing, obstructed challenge labels and missing required views. Clean photos and complete sets are included to expose false alarms. Reasonable differences on ambiguous examples are assessed using the team's explanation.

Live camera support is optional. It can contribute to the same usability and working-prototype criteria, but receives no separate bonus points. An upload prototype can earn the full score.

Submit through Devpost according to Bay Hacks' instructions. Nedas confirmed remote judging from 7:00 PM ET Saturday, September 19 through 10:00 AM ET Sunday, September 20, with submissions due at the start of that window. A live presentation or camera is not required by this track.

ACS has reserved additional evaluation inputs whose answers are not supplied to teams. Their use in remote judging, including how judges access a runnable prototype, must be agreed with the organizers and communicated to all teams before kickoff. Do not assume a live session or provide judges with personal credentials. All teams must receive the same evaluation procedure and equivalent time. Demonstration format/length and tie resolution remain subject to organizer confirmation. The proposed tie order is correctness, then retake guidance, followed by organizer adjudication.

The cash prize does not include an internship, employment or automatic adoption of the software. Refer questions about event eligibility, intellectual property and prize payment to the organizers; this guide does not create additional terms for those matters.
